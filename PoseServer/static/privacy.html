Gymie privacy

This pose engine is part of Gymie, a macOS menu bar app. It runs on your own Mac and listens only on 127.0.0.1.

Camera

  • During a break or practice, Gymie sends camera frames to this engine on your Mac to count reps and score form.
  • Frames are processed in memory and are never written to disk, logged, or sent anywhere else.
  • The blurred preview only changes what you see on screen; the engine needs the real frame to find your pose.

Stats

  • Your settings, XP, streaks and break history are stored in ~/Library/Application Support/Gymie on your Mac.
  • If you connect a GitHub repo, Gymie pushes your stats (display name, XP, level, streak, badges, daily break counts) to players/<your-login>.json in that repo, and reads the other files there to show the leaderboard. Keep that repo private. Anyone with access to it can see those stats.
  • GitHub is the only network service Gymie talks to.
